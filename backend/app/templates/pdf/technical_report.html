Understanding the Indicators

RSI (Relative Strength Index): Measures momentum. Below 30 = oversold (potential buy), above 70 = overbought (potential sell).

SMA (Simple Moving Average): Average price over period. Price above SMA is bullish, below is bearish.

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence): Trend-following indicator. MACD crossing above signal line is bullish.

Bollinger Bands: Volatility bands. Price at upper band = overbought, at lower band = oversold.

Stochastic: Momentum oscillator. Below 20 = oversold, above 80 = overbought.
